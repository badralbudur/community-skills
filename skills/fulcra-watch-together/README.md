# fulcra-watch-together

Pick a film two people will actually both enjoy, using what you have each really watched.

Everyone has had the standoff: two people, a streaming menu, forty minutes of scrolling, nothing agreed. This skill settles it with evidence instead of guesswork. Each person brings their own viewing history — Netflix, Letterboxd — and the agent looks for the overlap: the genres you both drift toward, the things one of you loves that the other has never tried but probably would.

You get recommendations with reasons attached, not a ranked list from nowhere.

**Both people have to say yes.** Nothing is read without an explicit, narrow share created by its owner, and either of you can revoke or leave that share at any time. Viewing history is personal, and it is treated that way: raw histories stay local, and one person's data is never written into the other's workspace.

Built on `fulcra-ingest`, `fulcra-analytics`, and `fulcra-workspaces`.
